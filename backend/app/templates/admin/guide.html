{# ───────── Paperback ───────── #}

Paperback orders, step by step

{{ icon('shield') }}

The golden rule

Only money showing in your bank account counts. Never a message, never a screenshot.

What the buyer does

  1. 1

    Chooses Delivery ({{ cfg.PAPERBACK_DELIVERY_PRICE|naira }}) or Pickup ({{ cfg.PAPERBACK_PICKUP_PRICE|naira }})

  2. 2

    Enters name, phone, email (and address) and places the order

  3. 3

    Gets an order code like BN-0007 + your bank details

  4. 4

    Transfers the money with BN-0007 as narration

  5. 5

    Taps "I've sent the money" on their order page with the payer's name & bank

What you do

  1. {{ icon('bell') }}

    Step 1

    Get notified

    A "New order" email arrives when someone orders (just for information). When they say they've paid, you get "Payment to confirm":

    {{ icon('mail') }}Payment to confirm: BN-0007

    Look for this transfer before acknowledging:

    Amount
    ₦24,000
    From
    ADEYEMI BOLA · Opay
    Narration
    BN-0007
  2. {{ icon('bank') }}

    Step 2 · the important one

    Check the money really arrived

    Open your bank app and find a credit where all of these match:

    {{ icon('check') }}AmountExactly copies × price
    {{ icon('check') }}Sender nameSame as the name they gave
    {{ icon('check') }}NarrationOrder code (some banks hide it)
    {{ icon('check') }}DateAfter the order was placed
  3. {{ icon('scale') }}

    Step 3

    Decide

    {{ icon('check') }} Money is there, amount is right

    Sign in here, open the order under Orders and tap Acknowledge payment.

    Buyer gets "Payment confirmed"

    {{ icon('x') }} No matching payment

    Open the order and click Payment not found.

    Buyer is asked to check, then confirm again on their order page

    {{ icon('alert') }} Wrong amount (e.g. underpaid)

    Message them with the WhatsApp link on the order page. Don't acknowledge until the full amount arrives.

    Nothing is sent until you act

    {{ icon('bank') }} Money arrived, but they never reported it

    You can still click Acknowledge payment on the order.

    Buyer gets "Payment confirmed"

  4. {{ icon('box') }}

    Step 4

    Supply the book

    {{ icon('truck') }} Delivery orders
    1. Mark out for delivery when the rider leaves. Put rider details in the note.
    2. Mark delivered once it arrives.
    {{ icon('store') }} Pickup orders
    1. Mark ready for pickup. The buyer is emailed the store address.
    2. Mark picked up when they collect (ask for their order code).

    Each click emails the buyer automatically. Cancel order is available any time before supply.
