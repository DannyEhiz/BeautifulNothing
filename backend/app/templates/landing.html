The book

Between what we believe and what we see, there is a passage. These stories walk it.

Beautiful Nothing is a collection of short stories exploring the clash of faith and the blurred line between the physical and the spiritual.

Across its varied tales, the characters are confronted with experiences that unsettle their beliefs, leaving them grappling with the possibility of alternative realities beyond what the eyes can see.

The author

Oladimeji Ramon

Journalist. Storyteller. Painter. Once, a veterinary surgeon.

Oladimeji Ramon is an award-winning journalist, fiction writer and part-time painter. He trained as a veterinary surgeon at the University of Ibadan and worked in veterinary practice for five years before switching careers in 2014 to pursue his lifelong passion for writing. He served as News Editor at The PUNCH newspaper and currently writes for The Africa Report, headquartered in Paris, France. Beautiful Nothing marks his debut in fiction.
